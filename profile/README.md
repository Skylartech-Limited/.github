# Skylartech Limited

Skylartech trains project professionals for PMI® certifications and provides project management, PMO and governance consulting. We are a PMI® Premier Authorized Training Partner (ATP No. C004927), and for over 12 years we have prepared professionals around the world for PMP®, CAPM® and other PMI® certifications.

[Website](https://www.skylartech.co.ke) | [Book training](https://www.skylartech.co.ke/book-training) | [Consulting](https://www.skylartech.co.ke/services) | [About us](https://www.skylartech.co.ke/about)

## What we offer

- **Training:** PMP®, CAPM®, PgMP®, PfMP®, PMI-ACP®, PMI-RMP®, PMI-PBA®, PMI-SP®, PMI-CP®, PMI-PMOCP®, PMI-CPMAI® and CSPP®, live online or on-site, so you can join from any country.
- **PDUs:** the [Strategic Project Leadership Masterclass](https://www.skylartech.co.ke/courses/strategic-project-leadership-masterclass) earns 59.75 PDUs toward renewing a PMI certification.
- **Consulting:** portfolio, PMO and governance, agile delivery, risk and compliance, and business analysis.
